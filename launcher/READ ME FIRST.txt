San Andreas x Skate 3 x MW2  -  PipeLink Launcher
====================================================

Skate and play Modern Warfare 2 inside GTA San Andreas. You need your OWN copies of the games:
nothing from any game is included here. The launcher builds the mod from your files.

WHAT YOU NEED
  - GTA San Andreas. The mod needs the classic version 1.0 (US). If you have the current Steam version,
    the launcher converts it for you ("Convert it" in step 1; downloads about 840 MB of patches).
    To undo that later: Steam > right-click the game > Properties > Installed Files > Verify integrity.
    Other versions (e.g. Rockstar Games Launcher) must be downgraded by hand with a GTA SA downgrader.
  - Skate 3 (optional): your Skate 3 Xbox 360 disc file (.iso). The free Skate 3 engine it runs on is
    downloaded by the launcher.
  - MW2 (optional): your Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2 game folder with the Multiplayer files.
  - Windows 10/11 64-bit and an internet connection for the first setup.

HOW TO USE
  1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere (e.g. your Desktop). Keep the "payload" folder next to the exe.
  2. Open PipeLinkLauncher.exe.
  3. Follow the three steps on screen until they show a green tick (Skate 3 and MW2 are optional).
  4. Press Install. The first time takes a few minutes.
  5. Press Play.

IN GAME
  F6   skate (Skate 3)
  F5   MW2 mode   -  Tab: scoreboard,  1-9 / mouse wheel: weapons,  5: killstreak
  A controller is recommended for skating.

UNINSTALL
  Open the launcher and click "Uninstall" (bottom right). Everything it changed in your games is put back.

TROUBLESHOOTING
  Click "Show details" in the launcher to see what it did. Error details are saved to
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\PipeLink\launcher_error.log. The game writes PipeLink.log next to PipeLink.asi.
